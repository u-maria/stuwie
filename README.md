# STUWIE

STUWIE is a simple and intuitive platform that helps students in Vienna find good places to study outside their homes.

Many students struggle to stay productive in their apartments. Finding a study-friendly café or library often turns into a long process of scrolling through social media, asking friends in group chats, or randomly testing places around the city.

Sometimes you arrive and discover there is no WiFi, no power outlets, or that laptops are not really welcome.

STUWIE was created to make this process easier. The platform collects study-friendly places in Vienna and presents them in a clear, structured, and easy-to-use interface so students can quickly find locations that actually work for studying.

This project is currently a demo MVP, but it represents the foundation of a potential student productivity platform.

Live demo  
https://stuwie--mariaum.replit.app/

## The idea

The idea behind STUWIE is simple.

Students should be able to answer the question

"Where can I go study right now?"

in less than 10 seconds.

Instead of researching locations manually, users can browse a curated collection of study spots and instantly see practical information that actually matters for studying.

The goal is to reduce friction and help students spend less time searching and more time being productive.

---

## Features

### Study spot directory

The platform provides a curated list of study locations across Vienna including cafés, libraries, and other study-friendly environments.

Each spot includes useful information such as

WiFi availability  
Power outlets  
Noise level  
Laptop friendliness  
Atmosphere tags  
Additional notes

This allows students to quickly understand whether a place fits their study style.

### Featured study spot

The homepage highlights a featured study spot that refreshes when the page reloads.

This small feature adds a sense of discovery and helps users quickly explore new locations.

### Filtering and discovery

Users can browse and filter study spots based on relevant conditions that matter when studying.

Instead of reading through dozens of reviews, students can immediately identify places that match their needs.

### Clean and intuitive interface

The interface focuses on clarity and simplicity.

The design aims to feel calm, modern, and easy to navigate so that users can find useful information without friction.

### Informational pages

The platform includes basic informational pages such as

About the project  
Legal notice / Impressum  
Privacy page

These elements reflect how the platform could operate as a real public website.

## Why this project exists

This project was inspired by a very real student problem.

As a Computer Science student living in Vienna, I often found it difficult to concentrate at home. Like many students, I rely on studying outside the apartment to stay focused and productive.

But finding a good place to work in the city can be surprisingly frustrating.

You might research a café online, arrive there, and realize

there are no power outlets  
the WiFi barely works  
laptops are discouraged  
the space is too loud for studying

After experiencing this problem repeatedly, I started thinking about how useful it would be to have a simple platform dedicated specifically to study-friendly locations.

STUWIE is my first step toward building that solution.

## Potential startup direction

Although STUWIE currently exists as a demo, the concept has strong potential to evolve into a real product.

Some possible future directions include

Community contributions where students can submit and review new study spots

Verified study-friendly locations where cafés and coworking spaces can receive a student-friendly badge

Sponsored listings where businesses promote their locations to students looking for study spaces

Premium features such as real-time occupancy indicators, crowd levels, or reservable study tables

University partnerships where universities integrate the platform to help students find study environments across the city

The long-term vision would be a platform that connects students with productive environments while also supporting local businesses that welcome them.
